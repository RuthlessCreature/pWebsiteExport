02 · Quote comparison

Ask every supplier for the same breakdown.

  • Unit price at each requested quantity
  • MOQ, tooling and other one-time costs
  • Sample cost, sample timing and production lead time
  • Included work, exclusions and assumptions
  • Packaging details, carton dimensions and gross weight
  • Buyer-requested test or document evidence
  • Payment proposal, quote validity and delivery term

Define the requirements for your product and destination. This tool organizes your request; it does not determine legal, customs, safety or certification requirements.
