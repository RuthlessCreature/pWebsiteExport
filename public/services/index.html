Services

From “find me a factory” to shipment-ready execution.

Pick one module or use us as a full China-side sourcing desk. Scope, responsibility, commercial terms and third-party fees are confirmed before work begins.

Core modules

Eight service blocks. One accountable workflow.

We deliberately separate sourcing, validation, quality and logistics. Mixing them into one vague “agent service” makes cost and accountability difficult to see.

Discuss your project →
01

Requirement engineering

Convert product photos, drawings, BOMs, reference links or rough ideas into a usable sourcing brief: specification, tolerance, material, certification, packaging, quantity, Incoterm and target timing.

02

Supplier discovery & screening

Search industrial clusters, B2B platforms, manufacturer networks and category-specific sources; eliminate obvious traders or capability mismatches when manufacturing ownership matters.

03

RFQ normalization & negotiation support

Put quotations on the same basis: tooling, MOQ, unit price, tax/export basis, packaging, payment, lead time, warranty, spare parts and exclusions. Commercial negotiation support can follow the normalized comparison.

04

Samples, prototypes & DFM follow-up

Coordinate samples and technical feedback, track revisions, clarify deviations and keep a written change log before mass production.

05

Supplier qualification & factory audit coordination

Review company identity, business scope, factory evidence, relevant certifications and process capability. On-site audit or specialist verification can be coordinated where justified.

06

Quality control & inspection coordination

Define incoming, in-process or pre-shipment checkpoints. Use drawings, golden samples, AQL or project-specific criteria as applicable. Independent third-party inspection can be arranged.

07

Consolidation & export documentation

Coordinate multiple supplier deliveries, shipping marks, packing lists, commercial invoices, export-ready packaging and handoff to the nominated freight forwarder.

08

Ongoing China procurement desk

For recurring purchasing, manage supplier communication, reorder tracking, alternates, replacement parts, new RFQs and issue escalation under one operating cadence.
