Services

From “find me a factory” to shipment-ready execution.

Pick one module or use us as a full China-side sourcing desk. Scope, responsibility, commercial terms and third-party fees are confirmed before work begins.

Core modules

Eight service blocks. One accountable workflow.

We deliberately separate sourcing, validation, quality and logistics so cost, technical risk and responsibility remain visible.

Discuss your project →
01

Requirement engineering

Convert product photos, drawings, BOMs, reference links or rough ideas into a usable sourcing brief.

02

Supplier discovery & screening

Search industrial clusters, B2B platforms and category-specific sources; eliminate capability mismatches early.

03

RFQ normalization

Put quotations on the same basis: tooling, MOQ, price, packaging, payment, lead time, warranty and exclusions.

04

Samples & DFM follow-up

Track sample revisions, technical feedback and deviations before production release.

05

Supplier qualification

Review company identity, factory evidence, relevant certifications and process capability.

06

Quality control

Define in-process or pre-shipment checkpoints based on drawings, samples, AQL or project-specific criteria.

07

Consolidation & export docs

Coordinate multiple supplier deliveries, packing lists, shipping marks, commercial documents and freight handoff.

08

Ongoing China procurement desk

For recurring purchasing, manage supplier communication, reorder tracking, alternates and exception handling.

Commercial models

Use the model that matches the job.

Exact fees depend on category complexity, supplier count, travel, inspection depth and transaction structure.

Fixed project fee

Best for a defined sourcing mission: shortlist, comparison, sample phase or one shipment.

Procurement service fee

Best when we manage supplier execution through purchase, QC, consolidation and export.

Monthly China desk

Best for companies with recurring RFQs, supplier follow-up and local coordination needs.
