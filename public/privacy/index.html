Privacy

Minimal, first-party measurement.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Contact form actions still open your email client; the website does not silently transmit the form fields to Pomerol.

What we collect

Cloudflare infrastructure may produce ordinary access logs. We also record a small set of first-party conversion events such as contact-page visits, WhatsApp/email/phone clicks and resource downloads. These events contain the page path, target type or resource path, page language, coarse country code and referring hostname. We do not place an analytics cookie and do not include names, email addresses, telephone numbers or inquiry text in these events.

Direct communications

If you email, call or message us, the information you choose to send becomes part of the communication record. This site does not ask you to upload sensitive sourcing documents directly through the page. If project documents are shared later, handling should be confirmed separately.
