How to use it

Compare evidence, not promises.

  1. Confirm the entity named on the quote and contract.
  2. Ask which processes are in-house and which are subcontracted.
  3. Match capability evidence to your actual product, drawing and acceptance criteria.
  4. Record gaps and owners before approving samples or payment milestones.

Adapt the checklist to the product, order value, customization and destination market. Not every item applies to every purchase.

Keep unresolved issues visible

Do not average away a critical mismatch.

A strong response on one topic does not resolve a different issue. Reconcile identity, bank beneficiary, production site, subcontracting, certificate scope and payment instructions separately with appropriate evidence.

The checklist helps organize review; it does not verify a company, inspect a factory, authenticate documents or guarantee product quality.

Scope and limits

Questions about supplier verification

Does completing this checklist verify or certify a supplier?

No. It structures a buyer's evidence review. The user must obtain and assess evidence from relevant official, supplier and independent sources. This page does not verify a company, factory, certificate, product or bank account.

Does a high number of completed items mean a supplier is safe?

No. The tracker counts review states and unresolved critical items; it does not calculate a quality score or recommend approval. Relevance and sufficiency of evidence depend on the exact product and transaction.

Should a trading company or subcontractor automatically be rejected?

No. A trading or multi-site arrangement may be legitimate. The buyer should understand the contracting entity, responsible parties, process ownership and controls for outsourced work before deciding whether the arrangement fits the project.

Where does my information go?

The checklist selections and notes are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to Pomerol. They are not stored after you leave the page. Export creates a CSV download only after you click the export button.
