Buyer challenge

Cameras, lenses, illumination, industrial computers, cables and custom mechanical parts had to work as one system, so compatibility mattered more than the lowest unit price.

China-side sourcing work

Built a line-by-line equivalence matrix, separated branded parts from acceptable alternates and coordinated optics and machined-part samples.

Control points

Electrical interfaces, lens mount, working distance, trigger protocol and mechanical datums were checked before alternates were approved.

Representative outcome

A consolidated 27-line BOM sourcing package was prepared with technical notes, supplier split and shipment plan.
