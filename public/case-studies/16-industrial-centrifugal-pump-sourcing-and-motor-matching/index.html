Buyer challenge

The distributor needed pump families that could cover several duty points without creating a new supplier for every project.

China-side sourcing work

Compared hydraulic ranges, motor options, seal materials, flange standards, documentation and spare-parts availability.

Control points

Duty point, motor efficiency, seal material and test-report format were frozen before quotation comparison.

Representative outcome

Three pump families were shortlisted with standardized data sheets and a repeat-order spare-parts matrix.

Detailed sourcing control plan

From requirement freeze to shipment handover.

This expanded control plan is a representative procurement method for this product category. It does not claim that every listed step was performed for a named client or shipment.

1. Requirement snapshot

  • Define duty point, flow, head, fluid, temperature, material compatibility, seal arrangement, motor efficiency, voltage/frequency and connection requirements.
  • Separate hydraulic performance requirements from motor, seal, coating, baseplate and accessory requirements.
  • State required test evidence and spare-parts expectations with the RFQ.

2. Supplier screening

  • Compare pump-curve capability, casting and machining control, balancing, hydrostatic or performance-test capability, motor sourcing and seal options.
  • Check whether quoted pump curves and motor selections correspond to the requested duty point rather than a generic catalog range.
  • Review material certificates, test formats and parts traceability for project-critical applications.

3. Validation gates

  • Verify selected operating point, motor margin, materials, seals, coatings and connection dimensions before approval.
  • Define production inspection for nameplate data, rotation, leakage, vibration/noise where applicable, accessories and packaging.
  • Freeze spare-parts BOM and documentation requirements before shipment.

4. Buyer handover pack

  • Approved pump-selection sheet and performance basis.
  • Material, motor and seal configuration record.
  • Inspection/test requirements, spare-parts list and shipment documents.
