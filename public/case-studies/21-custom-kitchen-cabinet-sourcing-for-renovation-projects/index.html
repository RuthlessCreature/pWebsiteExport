Buyer challenge

The contractor needed repeatable cabinet quality across different apartment layouts while keeping finish and hardware options controlled.

China-side sourcing work

Compared carcass materials, door finishes, hinges, drawer systems, worktop interfaces, flat-pack versus assembled packing and drawing approval flow.

Control points

Room-specific drawings, finish sample and hardware list had to match before production release.

Representative outcome

A modular cabinet specification was established for repeat renovation orders with fewer one-off decisions.

Detailed sourcing control plan

From requirement freeze to shipment handover.

This expanded control plan is a representative procurement method for this product category. It does not claim that every listed step was performed for a named client or shipment.

1. Requirement snapshot

  • Translate room drawings into cabinet module dimensions, material specifications, finish codes, hardware schedule, countertop interfaces and installation tolerances.
  • Control exposed finish, edge treatment and hardware brand/model separately from cabinet carcass construction.
  • Define room or unit labeling so mixed-project shipments remain installation-ready.

2. Supplier screening

  • Compare panel processing, edge banding, drilling, finishing, hardware integration and drawing/BOM control capability.
  • Review sample door and carcass workmanship under the actual finish specification rather than accepting catalog images.
  • Assess packing and module-identification methods for export damage and site sorting risk.

3. Validation gates

  • Approve shop drawings, finish sample, hardware schedule and critical module dimensions before batch production.
  • Use packing mock-up or defined corner/edge protection for vulnerable finished panels.
  • Control room/module labels and packing lists so installation teams can identify complete sets.

4. Buyer handover pack

  • Approved shop drawings, finish codes and hardware schedule.
  • Sample approval and packaging specification.
  • Room/module packing list and installation handover documentation.
