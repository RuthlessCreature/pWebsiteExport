Buyer challenge

The buyer needed consistent distributor kits containing components, printed packaging, accessory pouches and documentation.

China-side sourcing work

Sourced packaging converters and accessory suppliers, standardized kit contents, controlled artwork revisions and coordinated packing samples.

Control points

Each kit BOM was frozen before final assembly so missing low-value accessories could not delay complete cartons.

Representative outcome

A repeatable kitting and packaging workflow was created for distributor replenishment orders.
