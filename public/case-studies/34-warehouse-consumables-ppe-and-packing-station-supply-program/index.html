Buyer challenge

The operator needed recurring cartons, tapes, cutters, PPE, bins and packing-station accessories without creating separate import jobs for each category.

China-side sourcing work

Created category specifications, reorder levels and consolidation cadence; compared carton dimensions, material strength and PPE standards.

Control points

High-consumption items were separated from project-specific equipment so replenishment cadence stayed predictable.

Representative outcome

A recurring consolidated purchase cycle replaced ad-hoc emergency orders.
