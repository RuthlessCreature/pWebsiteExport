China procurement support from RFQ to shipment readiness.

Procurement from China is rarely one transaction. It is a chain of specifications, supplier decisions, samples, commercial terms, production milestones, inspection evidence and logistics handoffs. Pomerol International manages that chain as one procurement process.

Why buyers use us

The supplier search is only the first control point.

When procurement is fragmented across emails, marketplace chats and forwarders, buyers lose the single source of truth. We create a master requirement and supplier record so technical, commercial and logistics decisions remain traceable.

Useful for companies that do not need a full China office but do need China-side purchasing discipline across recurring orders, project procurement or multi-SKU programs.
