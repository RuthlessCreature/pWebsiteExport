What we do

A practical China-side sourcing and execution layer.

Platforms can show supplier names. The hard part is managing technical fit, quotation basis, sample revisions, quality gates, production follow-up and shipment readiness.

01

Supplier sourcing & RFQ

Turn drawings, photos, BOMs or rough requirements into a usable sourcing brief and comparable supplier quotations.

02

OEM / ODM coordination

Coordinate samples, tooling, packaging, labeling, variants and change control through the development cycle.

03

Qualification, QC & export

Verify suppliers, define inspection checkpoints, consolidate shipments and coordinate export documentation.

Working method

Start with the product. Build control around it.

A serious sourcing process starts from specifications and evidence, not a long list of factory links. We structure the requirement, compare suppliers on the same basis, close technical gaps, then control the path to shipment.
