What we do

Not a directory. A sourcing operating layer.

Alibaba and 1688 can show you suppliers. They do not own the messy work between “looks interesting” and “goods arrived as agreed.” That gap is where we work.

01

Supplier sourcing & RFQ

Translate the real requirement into a supplier brief, map Chinese manufacturing clusters, screen candidates, compare quotations and expose hidden commercial differences.

02

OEM / ODM coordination

Coordinate samples, drawings, packaging, labeling, product variants, tooling and change control so custom projects stay organized across teams and suppliers.

03

Supplier qualification

Verify the business entity, manufacturing capability, key processes and available evidence. Factory-audit and specialist testing can be coordinated when required.

04

Quality inspection

Define checkpoints before mass production, during production and before shipment. Inspection scope is tied to your specification, not generic checkbox theater.

05

Consolidation & export

Coordinate multi-supplier deliveries, packing lists, commercial documents, shipment readiness and forwarder handoff for air, sea, rail or courier routes.

06

China-side project desk

Need a local team to chase drawings, samples, lead times, replacement parts or a difficult factory? We can operate as the local extension of your purchasing team.

Execution model

Five gates between an idea and a shipment.

Every project does not need every service. The point is to make the gates explicit so commercial, technical and delivery risk do not get mixed together.

GATE 01

Define

Specification, quantity, target price, certification, market and delivery constraints.

GATE 02

Source

Supplier longlist, capability screen, quotation normalization and shortlist.

GATE 03

Validate

Samples, factory evidence, technical questions, commercial terms and risk review.

GATE 04

Control

PO alignment, production follow-up, change log, quality checkpoints and exceptions.

GATE 05

Deliver

Final inspection, export documents, consolidation, freight handoff and closeout.
