Why buyers use us

The supplier search is only the first control point.

Generic 'check quality' instructions create subjective inspections. The more technical the product, the more important it is to define critical dimensions, material evidence, electrical configuration, visual zones, test method and allowable deviation before production.

Useful for custom parts, electrical products, OEM goods, packaging, furniture, hospitality supplies and multi-factory consolidation programs.

Typical scope

  • Inspection checklist design
  • Approved sample and golden sample control
  • Dimensional and functional checkpoints
  • Packaging and labeling verification
  • Photo/video evidence package
  • Defect follow-up and reinspection
