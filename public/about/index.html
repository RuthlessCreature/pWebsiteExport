About

A small China-side team built for messy cross-border execution.

Pomerol International Trade (Zhuhai) Co., Ltd. / 波美猴国际贸易（珠海）有限公司 is based in Zhuhai, Guangdong, at the edge of one of the world’s densest manufacturing regions.

Our role

We are useful when the supplier exists, the product is possible, but the path from requirement to reliable delivery is still unclear.

Cross-border sourcing usually fails in the boring middle: ambiguous specs, quotations that are not comparable, untracked revisions, weak sample approval, silent substitutions, rushed inspections and logistics decisions made too late.

Our job is to make that middle visible and manageable. We coordinate the China side, document decisions and keep technical, commercial and delivery questions separated.

Operating principles

Less magic. More control.

Facts before promises

We do not treat a supplier profile, certificate screenshot or friendly chat as proof of capability.

Written specifications

If a requirement matters, it belongs in a drawing, BOM, checklist, sample reference or written acceptance.

Comparable quotations

Cheap price without aligned scope is not a saving; it is an accounting illusion waiting to become a dispute.

Exit options

We prefer sourcing structures that preserve alternates and avoid unnecessary dependence on one opaque vendor.

Geographic base

Zhuhai, connected to the Pearl River Delta.

Zhuhai provides fast access to manufacturing and logistics networks across Guangdong, including Zhongshan, Foshan, Guangzhou, Dongguan, Shenzhen and surrounding industrial clusters. Projects can also extend into the Yangtze River Delta and other specialized regions when the category demands it.

We work with overseas importers, distributors, project companies, brands and industrial buyers.
