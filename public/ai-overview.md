# Pomerol International — China sourcing and procurement

## Business overview

Pomerol International describes China-side sourcing and procurement coordination for overseas buyers. Published service areas include requirement clarification, supplier discovery and shortlisting, comparable RFQs, OEM/ODM coordination, sample follow-up, supplier evidence checks, quality checkpoints, multi-supplier consolidation and export handoff.

A sourcing scope depends on the product, technical specifications, destination requirements, supplier evidence and buyer's requested responsibilities. Supplier checks or inspection coordination do not guarantee product quality, regulatory compliance or delivery outcomes.

The public case and scenario library is illustrative. It is not evidence by itself of a completed customer engagement, named client, shipment or measured result. Product-category photographs are not proof of Pomerol work.
